Events ​

Available events ​

EventPayloadComment
plan_createdPlanSent after a plan is created.
plan_deletedPlanSent after a plan is deleted.
plan_updatedPlanUpdatedSent after a plan is updated.
sub_createdSubscriptionSent after a subscription is created.
sub_deletedSubscriptionSent after a subscription is deleted.
sub_updatedSubscriptionUpdatedSent after any changes in the subscription have occurred.
sub_pausedSubscriptionPausedSent after a subscription is paused.
sub_resumedSubscriptionResumedSent after a subscription is resumed.
sub_renewedSubscriptionRenewedSent after a subscription is renewed.
sub_expiredSubscriptionExpiredSent after a subscription is expired.
sub_usage_addedUsageSent after Usage was added to the subscription.
sub_usage_removedUsageSent after a subscription Usage was deleted.
sub_usage_updatedUsageUpdatedSent after any changes of the subscription Usage was occurred.
sub_discount_addedDiscountSent after Discount was added to the subscription.
sub_discount_removedDiscountSent after a subscription Discount was deleted.
sub_discount_updatedDiscountUpdatedSent after any changes of the subscription Discount was occurred.

Event example ​

json
{
  "type": "event",
  "code": "sub_created",
  "occurred_at": "2025-01-27T14:30:00+03:00",
  "payload": {
    "id": "8328c4b4-fa07-497b-9480-437559b8b136",
    "subscriber_id": "AnySubscriberID",
    "price": 100,
    "currency": "USD",
    "billing_cycle": "annual"
  }
}
